Privacy Policy — Son Pai Ruay Uploader

Last updated: 25 October 2026

  1. Who we are. Son Pai Ruay Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the “สอนไปเรื่อย” channel.
  2. Data we access from TikTok. With your permission (TikTok Login Kit), the tool receives an access token and reads your basic profile (user.info.basic: nickname, username, open ID) and your posting settings (creator info), and uses the video.publish permission to post the videos you choose.
  3. Where data is stored. Tokens are stored only on the owner’s own Windows PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. The tool has no server and no database. Nothing is sent to anyone except TikTok’s official API.
  4. What we do not do. We do not sell, share or rent any data. We do not track you, show ads, or collect data about other TikTok users.
  5. Videos and captions. Videos and captions are read from the owner’s own computer and sent only to TikTok when you press Post.
  6. Deleting your data. You can revoke the tool’s access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions). Deleting the token file on the PC removes all stored data.
  7. Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
