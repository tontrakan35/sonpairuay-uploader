Son Pai Ruay Uploader

Son Pai Ruay Uploader is a small desktop tool used by the Thai tutorial channel “สอนไปเรื่อย” (Son Pai Ruay) to post its own finished videos to its own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator finishes a video on their own Windows PC.
  2. The tool opens a local “Post to TikTok” page on the same PC (http://127.0.0.1), showing the creator’s TikTok nickname, a preview of the video, an editable caption, and the privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings.
  3. Nothing is sent to TikTok until the creator reviews everything, ticks the consent box and presses Post.
  4. The tool uploads the video with TikTok’s Content Posting API (Direct Post) and shows the post status.

The tool is used only by the channel owner, only for the channel owner’s own content.
